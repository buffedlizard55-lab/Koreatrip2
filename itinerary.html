How to use the status labels

User-supplied plan means the stop was in the itinerary notes, not that a reservation exists. Official detail verified refers only to the linked fact (for example, a hotel’s published check-in time). Unverified means no reliable exact value is asserted. Check current transport, holiday notices and opening details before travel.
