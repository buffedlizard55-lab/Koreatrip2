Verification summary

What checks out against official sources

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace: Nov–Feb hours 09:00–17:00, last admission 16:00, closed Tuesdays, adult admission 3,000 KRW — matches [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=87740).
  • Lotte Department Store Myeongdong: weekdays 10:30–20:00, weekends 10:30–20:30 — matches [Lotte official](https://m.global.lotteshopping.com/eng/store/main).
  • Olive Young Myeongdong: official hours 10:00–22:30 (itinerary says 8:00–midnight — conflict, flagged) — [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=148763).
  • N Seoul Tower: observatory weekdays 10:00–22:30 / weekends 10:00–23:00; adult ₩29,000; cable car 10:00–23:00 — [official site](https://www.nseoultower.co.kr/eng/visit/use.asp). The itinerary's "10:00–10:30" hours are incorrect.
  • AREX: Express ~43 min (T1) to Seoul Station, adult ₩13,000 — itinerary says 45 min / $7; fare does not match — [AREX official](https://www.airportrailroad.com/ticket/rsv).
  • Airport Limousine Bus: route 6015 to Myeongdong ~₩17,000 (~$12), 60–80 min — itinerary says $13 — [official site](https://www.airportlimousine.co.kr/en/sub/sub01.php).
  • KTX Seoul→Busan: ~2h15m–2h45m depending on stops; standard fare ~₩54,400–59,800 — [Korail](https://www.letskorail.com).
  • Starfield COEX Mall: 10:30–22:00 daily, open year-round — [official site](https://www.starfield.co.kr/coexmall/main.do).
  • Gwangjang Market food street: 09:00–23:00 daily; general market closed Sundays — [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=106345).
  • Haeundae Market: 09:00–22:00, varies by store — [Visit Busan](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000301003001000&uc_seq=294&lang_cd=en).
  • L7 Haeundae: 55 Haeun-daero 570beon-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan; ~1-minute walk to Haeundae Beach — [Lotte Hotels](https://www.lottehotel.com/).

Major irregularity — please review before traveling

Day 3 (Tuesday, Nov 3, 2026) schedules Gyeongbokgung Palace, which is closed on Tuesdays (the changing-of-the-guard ceremony is also suspended that day). The itinerary must be adjusted — e.g., move the palace visit to another day. See the Flagged for Review page for the full list, including the "Nov 2, 2006" typo and date/day mismatches on Day 1.
