## Limits

- Hours shown are published regular schedules; not a guarantee of service on travel day. Links should be revisited for closures/weather. Source pages sometimes return different content to crawlers or show dynamic “today” hours; do not translate those into guaranteed Nov 2026 hours.
- No flight, hotel, rail or restaurant booking confirmation was supplied. A recommendation is not a reservation.
- Day 7 specifies Itaewon only; Day 8 has no activities. Both remain flexible instead of inventing appointments.
- Many wish-list brands have no unambiguous current primary or government listing: status unverified, fields blank, and context-only official links provided for review.
