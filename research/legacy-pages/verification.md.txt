## Summary of irregularities (published on Flagged for Review page)
1. Day 3 (Tue Nov 3, 2026) schedules Gyeongbokgung Palace, which is closed Tuesdays. Must reschedule or swap days.
2. Day 2 date says "Nov 2, 2006" — should be 2026.
3. Day 1 calls Oct 31 a Sunday — Oct 31, 2026 is a Saturday (Nov 1 is Sunday).
4. N Seoul Tower hours in itinerary (10:00–10:30) conflict with official hours (weekdays 10:00–22:30, weekends 10:00–23:00).
5. Olive Young hours in itinerary (8:00–midnight) conflict with official (10:00–22:30).
6. AREX fare "$7" does not match official fares (Express ₩13,000 ≈ $9.30; all-stop ₩4,750 ≈ $3.40).
7. Limousine bus "$13" vs official ₩17,000 (~$12.10) standard / ₩18,000 (~$12.80) premium.
8. Gwangjang Market "some stalls until 2:00am" — official food street closes 23:00.
9. Many small venues (restaurants, cafes, shops) have no official English source; left blank/unverified — verify via Kakao Map / Naver Map or venue Instagram before visiting.
