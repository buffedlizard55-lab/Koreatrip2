2. **Third-party prices presented as ticket prices** — "$17.59 on Klook" (official ₩29,000) and "$15" aquarium (official ₩32,000 walk-up).
3. **Sky Capsule "round trip" instruction unconfirmed** — official English pages describe one-way boarding per capsule; fares per capsule, not per person. November hours 08:30–18:00 limit a "late afternoon" ride.
4. **Gamcheon November hours** — 09:00–17:00 in Nov–Feb; the plan's 2–3-hour wander must start by early afternoon.
5. **Gukje Market closure conflict** — official listings disagree (Sundays vs 1st/3rd Sundays); Wed visit unaffected.
6. **Coffee It House unverifiable** — no official listing; third-party hours conflict.
7. **L7 check-out 11:00 claim** — official check-out is 12:00.
8. **Taxi "1 hour / $20–30" to Busan Station** — no official source.
9. **"AREX to Hongdae"** — correct only via the All-Stop train; the Express does not stop at Hongik Univ.
10. **Days 13 & 15 unprogrammed**; Day 14 has only the evening show (official winter schedule 19:00 & 21:00 confirms the 7 pm plan).
